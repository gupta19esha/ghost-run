No fork reaches a unit on: reversal.

- reversal: the gap held on the other side for six months or more, and both that and the end gap are a unit or more.
- shock: months 21 to 24 moved the gap a unit more than months 18 to 21 did plus the gap already open at month 21 (forks at month 18 or earlier).
- quiet-money: a bar ends a unit apart while net worth ends close.
- trade-off: net worth and Safety or Freedom end a unit apart, in opposite directions.

Left out: the same trade-off on Life, 103 forks (4.1%), decisions D04 D19 D23 D25. Spending for Life is the trade the sheet writes into nearly every option; counted, it filled most of Spender's top five with "richer, enjoying less".

Real reversals are rare: 119 forks (4.7%) have a gap of half a unit on one side that ends a quarter unit on the other. 28 of them (1.1% of all forks) end with the ghost ahead after it was behind, the "looked worse, ended better" case.
